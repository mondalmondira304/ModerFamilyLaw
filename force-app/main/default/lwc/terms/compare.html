DISCLOSURE OF YOUR INFORMATION We understand the sensitive nature of family law matters. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. However, we may disclose your information in the following circumstances: 5.1 Disclosures Required by Family Law Proceedings Family law matters, including divorce, custody, and support proceedings, often require the disclosure of personal, financial, and other sensitive information. Depending on the jurisdiction and the nature of your case, we may be required to disclose your information in connection with: • Mandatory Financial Disclosures: Courts in each of our practice states require parties to exchange financial disclosures, including income, assets, debts, and expenses. These disclosures are mandated by statute and may become part of the court record. • Custody and Parenting Evaluations: Information relevant to custody, parenting time, and the best interests of the child may be disclosed to courts, guardians ad litem, child and family investigators, custody evaluators, parenting coordinators, and other court-appointed professionals. • Court Filings and Public Records: Pleadings, motions, affidavits, exhibits, financial disclosures, and other documents filed with the court may become part of the public record unless sealed by court order. We take reasonable steps to redact sensitive information such as Social Security numbers and financial account numbers where permitted by court rules. • Discovery Obligations: Responses to subpoenas, interrogatories, requests for production, and depositions may require the disclosure of personal information to opposing parties and their counsel. • Mediation and Alternative Dispute Resolution: Information may be shared with mediators, arbitrators, and collaborative law professionals in connection with alternative dispute resolution processes. 5.2 Other Permitted Disclosures We may also disclose your personal information to: • Service Providers and Experts: Experts, consultants, investigators, forensic accountants, appraisers, and other professionals engaged in connection with your matter • Legal and Regulatory Requirements: Law enforcement agencies, government regulators, or courts when required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of firm assets, provided that the receiving entity agrees to protect your information consistent with this Privacy Notice and applicable professional obligations • Protection of Rights and Safety: To prevent imminent harm, protect our legal rights, or as otherwise permitted by the applicable rules of professional conduct (e.g., ABA Model Rule 1.6(b)) 6.
